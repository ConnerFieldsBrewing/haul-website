Good to know

Questions we get

Where is The Haul?

121 SW H Street in the historic district of downtown Grants Pass, Oregon. Phone (541) 474-4991.

What are The Haul's hours?

Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday 11 am to 8 pm; Friday and Saturday 11 am to 9 pm; closed Tuesdays. Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas.

What is The Haul known for?

Wood-fired pizza baked at about 750°F over madrone in an oven from Naples, double smash burgers, meats smoked in house, and tacos on corn tortillas we make from scratch. Everything is made by hand, with a full bar and cold beer.

Does The Haul have vegan and gluten-free food?

Yes. Vegan options include the Vegan pizza, falafel, veggie burger, tempeh Reuben, cauliflower and bean tacos and several salads, and any veggie pizza can get vegan cheese. Gluten-free crust and buns are available, and our corn tortilla tacos are gluten-free.

Can I order online for pickup?

Yes. Order online for pickup at 121 SW H Street, paid through Square. Online orders close 30 minutes before we do.

Can I book a private party at The Haul?

Yes. Our upstairs room hosts birthdays, rehearsal dinners, team parties and fundraisers with food and bar packages from our kitchen. Send an event request and we reply with rates the same day.

Is The Haul good for kids?

Yes, we have a kids menu with grilled cheese, buttered parmesan noodles and a quesadilla.

Does The Haul cater events?

Our mobile kitchens and taps (Pizza Haul, BBQ Haul, Beer Haul and Haul Dogs) come to markets, festivals, weddings and private parties around Southern Oregon. Email haulevents@gmail.com.
