Scratch kitchen & taproom in downtown Grants Pass

Everything here is made by hand, from scratch: pizza fired over madrone, meats smoked in house, tortillas from corn we nixtamalize ourselves. It's a wide-ranging menu with something for everyone, including vegan, gluten-free and kids' options. But the food is only half of it. The Haul is where downtown gathers for live music, long tables and good company.
